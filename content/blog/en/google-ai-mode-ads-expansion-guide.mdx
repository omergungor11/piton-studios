Google's AI-powered search experience, AI Mode, answers long, conversational questions. On 28 October 2026 Google
opened the ads shown inside those answers to 10 new countries outside the US. Türkiye is not on that list, but that does
not mean advertisers in Türkiye can ignore it: AI Overviews are live in Türkiye and search ads can already appear around
those answers. This post covers what changed, how ads are matched and what to prepare for today. If you are weighing
paid ads against organic search, see the
[related question on our FAQ page](/en/faq#faq-google-ads-vs-seo).

## What was announced?

[According to Google's 28 October announcement](https://blog.google/products/ads-commerce/ads-in-ai-mode-global/), ads
inside AI Mode opened in Australia, Canada, India, Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan, the Philippines and
Singapore. Google says it plans to expand to Brazil, Mexico and the United Kingdom in the last quarter of 2026; that is a
plan, not a launch that has happened. In the same announcement Google started "multiturn" ads, which can appear in
follow-up questions, in the US only. Ads carry a "Sponsored" label.

A hint as to why Google is expanding now sits in its own numbers.
[As reported by The Keyword (thekeyword.co)](https://www.thekeyword.co/news/google-ads-in-ai-mode-expansion), Google's
search revenue grew 19 percent in the first quarter of 2026, 17 percent in the second and 12 percent in the third.
Philipp Schindler, Google's chief business officer, described the step as taking this ad revenue "global for the first
time".

## How far have AI Mode ads spread?

AI Mode ads went from one country to eleven in a little over a year.
[According to Search Engine Roundtable](https://www.seroundtable.com/google-expands-ai-mode-42412.html), Google first
started testing ads in AI Mode in the US in May 2025. The October 2026 expansion took that to eleven; if the
last-quarter plan goes ahead it will reach fourteen.

Australia, Canada, India, Indonesia (AI Overviews only), Kenya, Malaysia, New Zealand, Nigeria, Pakistan (text ads
only), the Philippines and Singapore; the language is English only. The page also notes the list "may not be
exhaustive".

## What is the situation in Türkiye?

Türkiye and Turkish are not on the list for ads inside AI Mode and AI Overviews. But the help page describes two
separate placements, and the second one does cover Türkiye.

| Placement | Where it is available | Türkiye |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inside AI Overviews and AI Mode answers | US and 11 English-speaking markets, English only | No |
| Above or below AI Overviews | 200+ countries and territories where AI Overviews are offered, all languages | Yes |
| AI Mode follow-up questions (multiturn) | US only | No |

AI Overviews have been shown in Türkiye since February 2026, as we covered in our
[post on Google's AI search controls](/en/blog/google-ai-search-controls-ai-overviews-ai-mode). So a search campaign
in Türkiye can already appear above or below an AI answer today. Ads placed inside the answer have not arrived yet.

<Callout type="info" title="The timeline is unclear, the preparation is not">
  Google has not given a date for Türkiye. But when ads inside the answer arrive, existing campaigns will be eligible
  automatically and cannot be switched off separately. So preparing means making today's campaign settings solid.
</Callout>

## How is an ad matched to an AI answer?

The help page describes two matching methods. **Direct query matching** works like the classic results page on queries
with clear purchase intent. **Contextual matching** applies to longer, exploratory questions and considers the query and
the AI's answer together.

| Campaign or match type | Direct query matching | Contextual matching |
| --- | --- | --- |
| AI Max for Search | Yes | Yes |
| Broad match (Search) | Yes | Yes |
| Shopping campaigns | Yes | Yes |
| Performance Max | Yes | Yes |
| Exact and phrase match | Only with clear commercial intent | No |

This table points to an important conclusion: the campaigns that reach long, exploratory questions in AI Mode are the
types where AI broadens targeting. We covered the automatic move of search campaigns to AI Max in our
[AI Max migration guide](/en/blog/google-ads-ai-max-migration-guide); both changes point the same way.

## What changes for advertisers?

The biggest change is the limit on control. According to the help page, ads cannot be targeted only to AI Overviews or
AI Mode, and you cannot opt out of these placements separately. The controls that remain are negative keywords and
brand exclusions. Billing follows the same search auction as before; there is no extra charge.

The second change is the ad copy itself. Google adds short explanations generated by Gemini next to some ads. According
to the help page, advertisers cannot write, edit, approve or turn off these explanations. Google also says paid ads do
not influence organic AI answers.

The third is measurement. Results from these ads are added to standard campaign reports in aggregate, with no separate
AI Mode breakdown.
[In PPC Land's assessment](https://ppc.land/advertisers-in-10-more-countries-cant-opt-out-of-googles-ai-mode-ads/), that
means advertisers cannot separate the performance of AI Mode traffic from other search traffic.

## What do the first data points say?

The data Google has shared is limited. According to the announcement, AI Mode queries are on average three times longer
than classic search, and sponsored and organic items in AI Mode product listings have a "similar" click-through rate.
That click comparison rests on 15 days of US data, and Google did not publish the rates themselves. Search Engine
Roundtable also notes that product results are visual, and that it is unclear how text ads perform.

There is an independent measurement too. According to the Productrise study reported by PPC Land, across more than
100,000 queries tracked for 21 days in the US and the UK in July 2026, AI Mode showed products on about 23 percent of
shopping queries; in classic search the figure was about 88 percent. The average number of product listings per page was
4.3 in AI Mode and 22.5 in classic search.

## What you can do now

<Callout type="warn" title="Manage a placement you cannot switch off with negatives">
  Because you cannot opt out of AI Mode and AI Overviews separately, the way to stay away from unwanted queries is
  negative keywords and brand exclusions. If you use broad match or AI Max, review the search terms report regularly.
</Callout>

| Step | What to do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search terms | Review the report weekly and add irrelevant queries as negatives | Contextual matching can bring longer, different queries |
| Match strategy | Use broad match and AI Max deliberately | These are the types that reach long AI questions |
| Landing pages | Make sure each ad's page clearly answers the question | A visitor from a long question wants detail |
| Product data | Keep the Merchant Center feed complete | Shopping ads can also appear inside the answer |
| Conversion tracking | Check conversions against site and order data | Reports have no AI Mode breakdown |
| Organic visibility | Structure content so AI answers can cite it | Ads do not influence the organic answer |

Do not neglect the organic side: according to Google, ads do not influence the organic AI answer, so appearing as a
source in the answer itself is separate work. We covered that in our
[post on search visibility in the AI era](/en/blog/search-visibility-in-the-ai-era-seo-to-geo).

## Common mistakes

- **Saying "it's not in Türkiye, so it doesn't concern me".** Ads above and below AI Overviews already apply in
  Türkiye, and when ads inside the answer arrive, existing campaigns will be eligible automatically.
- **Looking for an off switch.** There is no separate opt-out; control comes through negative keywords and exclusions.
- **Misreading aggregated reports.** Because AI Mode traffic is not shown separately, sudden changes in cost or
  conversions call for a look at the search terms report.
- **Generalising Google's click comparison.** "Similar click-through rate" is 15 days of US data with no published
  rates; see your own account's result in your own data.
- **Relying on ads alone.** Since ads do not influence the organic answer, appearing in AI answers takes separate
  content and SEO work.

## Conclusion

Google has started taking ads inside AI answers global. Türkiye is not on the list yet, but search campaigns already
appear around AI Overviews, and when ads inside the answer arrive, existing campaigns will be eligible automatically.
So the work for today is to tighten negative keywords, landing pages and conversion tracking so that you can manage a
placement you cannot switch off.
