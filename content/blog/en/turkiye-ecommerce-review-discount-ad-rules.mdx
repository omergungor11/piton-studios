In an online store, the review section, the discount label and the ad creatives are no longer just a marketing
matter in Türkiye. An amendment to the Regulation on Commercial Advertising and Unfair Commercial Practices came
into force on 1 August 2026, and in October the Ministry of Trade published inspection results that also cover
these rules. With Black Friday (27 November) close, discount ads and product reviews are among the areas most
likely to be checked. This post covers what changed, how large the sanctions are and what you need to change
technically on your site. We briefly explain what conversion rates depend on in our
[FAQ](/en/faq#faq-conversion-optimization).

## What changed?

[According to the Ministry of Trade's announcement of 1 July 2026](https://ticaret.gov.tr/haberler/ticaret-bakanligi-tarafindan-ticari-reklam-ve-haksiz-ticari-uygulamalar-yonetmeliginde-yapilan-degisikliklerle-tuketicilerin-aldatici-reklam-ve-ticari-uygulamalara-karsi-korunmasi-guclendiriliyor),
the amendment was published in the Official Gazette that day and came into force on 1 August 2026. Its scope is
broad: consumer reviews, discount advertising, AI-generated content, social media influencers, targeted ads,
environmental claims and the use of academic titles are all covered in the same package.

The table below collects the items that create the most work for e-commerce sites. Article numbers and details
come from [Hukuki Haber's commentary](https://www.hukukihaber.net/ticari-reklam-ve-haksiz-ticari-uygulamalar-yonetmeliginde-yapilan-degisiklikler/amp)
and [Cottgroup's summary](https://cottgroup.com/tr/mevzuat/item/ticari-reklam-ve-haksiz-ticari-uygulamalar-yonetmeliginde-degisiklik-yapildi).

| Topic | New rule | Where it affects your site |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Consumer reviews | Only buyers may review; a review whose purchase cannot be verified may not be published | Review form, order system |
| Review lifetime | Stays online for at least one year, whether positive or negative | Review archive, deletion rules |
| Review order | Sorted by an objective criterion such as date or rating | Product page, filters |
| Objection period | Cut from 72 to 48 hours; with no response, the review is published | Seller panel, notifications |
| Discount ads | Previous price = lowest price of the last 10 days | Price history, campaign label |
| AI | Digital characters indistinguishable from humans must be clearly disclosed | Ad creatives, product videos |
| Influencers | "Advertising" or "promotion" label on paid posts | Social media partnerships |
| Targeted ads | Users must be informed; profiling aimed at children is banned | Ad and cookie settings |

## Consumer reviews: real buyers only

The most visible effect is in the review section. According to the Ministry, reviews collected from channels
where the purchase process cannot be verified may not be published. Reviews under different headings, such as
product, service, delivery and seller, must also be shown together in a single area, in a clear and
distinguishable way.

Legal commentaries add detail: the right to review must be given only to consumers who bought the product or
service, reviews must stay online for at least one year whether positive or negative, and they must be sorted by
an objective criterion. Agreements to obtain fake or approving reviews are also banned.
[As CHIP Online reports](https://chip.com.tr/guncel/e-ticarette-sahte-yorum-donemi-kapaniyor-yeni-kurallar-yururlukte_184347.html),
negative reviews cannot be deleted or hidden just because they are negative, and review order cannot be changed
to push a product or seller.

The seller's objection or response period was cut from 72 to 48 hours. If there is no response in that time, the
review is published directly. Leaving review notifications in the seller panel for days no longer keeps a review
offline; once the period ends, the review appears on its own.

<Callout type="warn" title="Rejecting a review needs a reason">
  According to Alomaliye's report on Advertising Board decisions, one company was fined 2,167,412 lira for
  rejecting a consumer review without a general and concrete reason. If you moderate reviews, record the reason
  for every rejection.
</Callout>

## Why do reviews matter so much?

The rules are strict because reviews carry so much weight in buying decisions.
[According to the Spiegel Research Center](https://spiegel.medill.northwestern.edu/how-online-reviews-influence-sales/)
at Northwestern University, a product with five reviews is 270% more likely to be bought than one with no
reviews. When reviews are shown, the conversion rate rises 190% for a lower-priced product and 380% for a
higher-priced one. A verified buyer badge makes a review more credible and improves the odds of purchase by 15%.

## Discount ads: the lowest price of the last 10 days

According to the Ministry, the previous price in a discount ad must be the lowest price applied in the last ten
days before the discount starts. For perishable goods such as fruit and vegetables, and for services, the price
immediately before the discount is used. According to Cottgroup's summary, if you sell through several channels,
only the channel running the discount counts; according to Hukuki Haber, the rules also cover loyalty programmes
and conditional campaigns.

In its [discounted sales warning of July 2025](https://www.forbes.com.tr/ekonomi/ticaret-bakanligi-ndan-indirimli-satis-uyarisi),
the Ministry had asked sellers to use the lowest price of the 30 days before the discount. The 2026 sources we
reviewed do not explain how the two periods relate. The practical answer is to keep at least 30 days of price
history and calculate the previous price on the campaign label from that record automatically; that way your
record is ready whichever period is asked about.

<Callout type="tip" title="Don't enter the previous price by hand">
  Filling in the "previous price" field on a campaign label by hand is the most common source of errors. A record
  that stores price changes with their dates, and a label calculated from it, also leaves a trail you can defend
  in an inspection.
</Callout>

The Ministry's announcement of 16 October shows how this rule is used in inspections: ads that did not set the
pre-discount price from the lowest price of the last ten days and used claims such as "up to 75% off" were found
in breach. If you plan your Black Friday campaigns with the timeline in our
[November sales readiness post](/en/blog/november-sales-ecommerce-readiness-guide), remember to fix your price
record at least ten days before the campaign starts.

## AI, influencers and targeted ads

The amendment also sets explicit rules for AI-generated advertising content for the first time. According to the
Ministry, digital characters that cannot be told apart from real people must be clearly disclosed in ads, and
digital copies that give the impression a real person used or recommends a product are banned. If you use an
AI-generated "customer" or "expert" in product videos or social media ads, you need to add that information
visibly. We covered similar transparency obligations in the European Union in our
[EU AI Act post](/en/blog/eu-ai-act-transparency-rules-chatbots-ai-content).

In partnerships with social media influencers, paid posts must carry an "advertising" or "promotion" label;
according to Cottgroup, live streams must disclose this at the start. For targeted ads, users must be informed,
and profiling aimed at children is banned.

## How much enforcement is there?

The Advertising Board publishes its meeting results regularly. According to the Ministry, the Board reviewed more
than 33,000 applications in the first nine months of 2026 and imposed more than 249 million lira in
administrative fines. [According to Alomaliye](https://www.alomaliye.com/2026/08/19/reklam-kurulundan-aldatici-reklamlara-dev-ceza/),
the total for the first eight months was 218.4 million lira.

## What should change on your site?

Most of these rules are easy to miss when left to a person's attention. The safest approach is to build them into
the store's software.

| When | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Now | Open the review form only for delivered orders; stop guest reviews and imports from outside sources |
| Now | Tie review order to an objective criterion such as date or rating; review any "featured review" logic |
| Now | Remove deletion or make a recorded reason mandatory; archive reviews for at least one year |
| 10+ days before a campaign | Keep a dated price history and calculate the previous price from it automatically |
| Before a campaign | Check discount claims ("up to 75% off") against the real number of products |
| Ongoing | Set up notifications and reminders for the 48-hour period in the seller panel |
| Ongoing | Disclose AI-generated images and videos, and label influencer posts as advertising |

## Common mistakes

- **Importing old reviews from outside sources.** Reviews whose purchase cannot be verified may not be published;
  moving marketplace or social media reviews to your site can break this rule.
- **Quietly hiding negative reviews.** Deleting a review just because it is negative, or pushing it to the end of
  the list, is banned; moderation needs a recorded reason.
- **Entering the "previous price" on campaign day.** The previous price must come from the record of the ten days
  before the campaign; a price raised just before the campaign makes the discount look bigger than it is.
- **Using AI images without disclosure.** Digital characters that cannot be told apart from real people must be
  clearly disclosed in ads.
- **Trying to solve the rules with an instruction alone.** Review and price rules are applied thousands of times a
  day; a rule that isn't built into the software will be missed sooner or later.

## Conclusion

The 1 August 2026 amendment ties trust signals in Turkish e-commerce to records: a review comes only from a real
buyer and stays online for at least one year, and the previous price in a discount ad comes from the record of
the last ten days. The Advertising Board has applied these rules from the first months, and Black Friday is when
discount ads are checked most closely.
