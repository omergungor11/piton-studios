Taxpayers on the simplified method and the business account basis were the last holdouts for paper invoices in
Türkiye. In 2025 and 2026 these businesses could issue paper invoices up to 3,000 TL (about €67 at 1 € = 45 ₺); from
1 January 2027 that limit ends and every invoice will be issued as an e-Archive invoice. A new communiqué published
on 24 November 2026 also widened the systems invoices can be issued from. This post covers who is affected, what
changes, and how businesses selling online should prepare their order and invoice flow. If you are curious about
payment and marketplace integrations, see the [related question on our FAQ page](/en/faq#faq-marketplace-integration).

## What is changing?

e-Archive invoice rules are set out in Tax Procedure Law General Communiqué No. 509. Communiqué No. 589, published on
31 December 2025, put the phasing out of paper invoices on a timetable.
[According to TÜRMOB's note](https://www.turmob.org.tr/ekutuphane/Read/9208c1d3-f311-468d-9dd7-0d50a202bd6c),
simplified-method and business account taxpayers may issue paper invoices up to 3,000 TL between 1 January 2025 and
31 December 2026, and must issue invoices above that amount as e-Archive invoices. From 1 January 2027 all invoices,
whatever the amount, will be e-Archive. Invoices printed by tradesmen's chambers also cannot be used after
31 December 2026.

| Taxpayer group | 2026 | From 1 January 2027 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Balance-sheet basis | All invoices e-Archive (no amount limit) | No change |
| Simplified method and business account | Paper up to 3,000 TL, e-Archive above | All invoices e-Archive |
| Tradesmen's chamber invoice | Usable for simplified method and business account | Not usable |
| Buyer is an e-Invoice user | e-Invoice | e-Invoice |

[Korgun's guide](https://www.korgun.com.tr/blog/2027-e-arsiv-fatura-zorunlulugu-kimler-kapsamda) points to two
practical rules as well: an invoice must be issued within seven days of delivery of the goods or performance of the
service, and an e-Archive invoice can be cancelled within eight days of transmission, after which a return or
correction document is needed.

## What did the new 24 November communiqué bring?

Tax Procedure Law General Communiqué No. 598 was published in Official Gazette No. 33440 on 24 November 2026 and takes
effect in full on 1 January 2027.
[As reported by Habere Güven](https://www.habereguven.com/e-arsiv-faturada-yeni-duzenleme-1-ocak-2027de-neler-degisecek),
it amends various provisions of Communiqué No. 509. The main points:

- e-Archive invoices can also be issued through secure mobile payment and electronic document management systems,
  taxi fiscal devices and new-generation cash registers authorised to issue e-documents.
- On these devices, the device's fiscal certificate replaces the issuer's signature.
- Paper documents can be issued in documented cases such as device failure or theft, or failure or theft of the
  fiscal seal or electronic signature tool.
- Physicians can issue e-Self-Employment Receipts from qualifying new-generation devices; provided they keep the
  device at the workplace, their separate obligation to keep POS devices ends. Habere Güven stresses that this is not an
  unconditional exemption.

[ABC Gazetesi's report](https://abcgazetesi.com.tr/ekonomi/resmi-gazetede-yayimlandi-fatura-ve-odeme-sistemlerinde-1-ocak-2027de-yeni-donem-878210)
also says the communiqué has 16 articles and that simplified-method and business account taxpayers will issue
e-Archive invoices "without an amount limit" from 1 January 2027.

## How far has e-transformation come?

The end of paper invoices is the last step of a transformation that has run for years.
[According to TÜRKKEP's report based on Revenue Administration data](https://turkkep.com.tr/milyonlarca-mukellef-e-belge-uygulamalarini-kullaniyor/),
the number of taxpayers using e-Invoice rose from 187,597 in 2019 to 1,107,692 in 2023.
[According to Anadolu Agency's compilation of Revenue Administration data](https://www.memurlar.net/haber/1163301/kagit-fatura-tarih-oluyor-e-fatura-kullanici-sayisi-2-milyona-yaklasti.html),
the figure reached 1,565,603 in 2024 and 1,937,308 in 2025. The number of e-Ledger users rose from 781,584 in 2024 to
2,192,798 in 2025.

## What is the difference between e-Archive and e-Invoice?

Both are electronic invoices, but they are split by buyer. If the buyer is also an e-Invoice user, the invoice is an
e-Invoice and is delivered to the other party through the Revenue Administration's system. If the buyer is not an
e-Invoice user, for example a final consumer, the invoice is issued as an e-Archive invoice and delivered to the buyer
by email or as a printout. Because most online store orders go to final consumers, e-Archive is the everyday document
of e-commerce.

[According to Mysoft's assessment based on the Revenue Administration's 2025 statistics](https://mysoft.com.tr/turkiye-de-e-donusum-rekor-kirdi-2025-te-e-fatura-ve-e-arsiv-zirveye-ulasti),
about 1.2 billion e-Invoices and 12 billion e-Archive invoices were issued in 2025, or around 32 million e-Archive
invoices a day. These figures come from a software company; we could not open the Revenue Administration's own
publication.

## What does it mean for a business selling online?

For a tradesperson issuing a few invoices a month, the Revenue Administration's free e-Archive portal may be enough.
But for a business selling online regularly, typing every order into the portal by hand takes time and raises the
risk of missing the seven-day issuing window. At that point the invoice needs to be part of the order system.

Points to watch in this flow:

- **Document type:** the system should choose e-Invoice or e-Archive depending on whether the buyer is an e-Invoice
  user. This check matters especially on a B2B site selling to companies.
- **Timing:** the invoice must be issued within seven days of delivery or service; an automatic flow can tie this to the
  order status.
- **Cancellation and returns:** an e-Archive invoice can be cancelled within eight days, after which a return document
  is needed. The returns process in the order system should make this distinction.
- **Marketplaces and channel consistency:** if a marketplace, your own site and in-store sales issue invoices from
  different places, numbering and reporting need to be brought together in one place.

## Common mistakes

- **Saying "my invoices are small, I'm under the limit".** The 3,000 TL limit ends on 31 December 2026; in 2027 the
  amount does not matter.
- **Leaving it to the last week.** Choosing an integrator, getting a fiscal seal or e-signature and integrating the site
  take several steps, and they can drag on in the year-end rush.
- **Typing every order into the portal by hand.** As volume grows, so does the risk of errors and delays; the seven-day
  window can be missed.
- **Fixing the document type.** Issuing e-Archive to every buyer means the wrong document for a business customer that
  uses e-Invoice.
- **Forgetting returns.** Since cancellation isn't possible after eight days, the return document flow has to be defined
  in advance.

## Conclusion

On 1 January 2027 paper invoices end in Türkiye for simplified-method and business account taxpayers too, and the
communiqué published on 24 November widens the systems e-Archive invoices can be issued from. For a business selling
online this is not only about meeting a legal requirement; it is about making the invoice a natural step of the order
and cutting manual work and error risk. There is less than a month left.
