ChatGPT is no longer just an alternative to search; it is also an advertising channel. OpenAI's
self-service ad platform, Ads Manager, has been open to businesses in Türkiye since September 2026.
This post explains where and to whom ads appear in ChatGPT, how targeting and billing differ from
Google Ads, how large the audience in Türkiye has become, and which businesses should test it now.
Organic visibility in AI assistants is a separate topic; we gave the short answer
[in our FAQ](/en/faq#faq-llm-visibility).

## How ChatGPT ads reached Türkiye

OpenAI [began testing ads in ChatGPT in the US on 9 February 2026](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt).
Ads Manager for small and medium-sized businesses was introduced in May, and on 31 August
[self-service access opened across Europe, India, the Middle East and North Africa](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/).
In the same announcement, OpenAI said ad revenue reached a $1 billion annualised run rate in under
200 days, that tens of thousands of advertisers use the platform, and that the free tier serves more
than 1 billion weekly active users.

Türkiye was not on the list at that point.
[Digital Applied's 3 September compilation](https://www.digitalapplied.com/blog/where-chatgpt-ads-are-sold-every-market-and-date)
showed Türkiye outside the self-service list. Today
[OpenAI's availability page](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001245-ads-manager-availability)
lists Türkiye as "Available", among 63 countries in total;
[Turkish guides](https://www.ticimax.com/blog/chatgpt-reklamlari-turkiyede-acildi-reklamverenler-icin-rehber)
report this status from 17 September 2026.

One condition matters: [Ads Manager accounts](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001213-ads-manager-beta-account-setup)
are for businesses only. Individual accounts are not supported, agencies cannot create accounts on a
client's behalf and are invited to the client's account instead, and the legal entity that
advertises and is billed must be based in a listed country.

## Where ads appear and who sees them

[According to OpenAI's help article](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt),
an ad appears as a single unit below ChatGPT's answer; it can contain one or more images and is
always labelled as an ad. A multi-image format is also being tested during image generation.

| Who sees ads | Who does not |
|---|---|
| Users on the Free and Go plans | Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts |
| Adult users whose conversation context suits an ad | Accounts identified as under 18 |
| Users with ad personalisation on or off (if off, only the current chat is used) | Health, mental health and political conversations, and temporary chats |

Political advertising is not currently allowed, and every ad goes through a policy review before it
runs. The table makes one thing clear: a ChatGPT ad appears right below the answer, at the moment a
user is researching a topic. That moment is closer to a search ad than to a social media feed ad.

## Targeting: no keywords, only context

This is the biggest difference. In Google Ads you choose the searches you appear on with keywords.
In ChatGPT, the ad is selected based on the conversation's context, the ad's content and the
"context hints" the advertiser provides. When several ads qualify, relevance and the bid decide the
order.

| Setting | Google search ad | ChatGPT ad |
|---|---|---|
| When it appears | On a specific search query | When the conversation's context fits |
| Main targeting | Keywords and match types | Context hints, country, platform, custom audience |
| Negative keywords and search terms report | Yes | No |
| Location | Country, city, region | Country; region, city and postal code for campaigns without a product feed |
| Custom audience (email / phone list) | Yes | Yes; at least 25,000 matches to include |

[According to the campaign creation guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001210-create-campaigns-for-chatgpt-ads),
platforms can be chosen across the Android and iOS apps and desktop and mobile web. Campaigns built
on a product feed can only be targeted at country level.
[Ticimax's guide](https://www.ticimax.com/blog/chatgpt-reklamlari-turkiyede-acildi-reklamverenler-icin-rehber)
also stresses that keyword, negative keyword and interest targeting are not available.

<Callout type="warn" title="The 25,000-match threshold can block small businesses">
  Using a custom audience for inclusion requires at least 25,000 matched users. A business whose
  customer list is smaller can only use custom audiences for exclusion; the real targeting is done
  through context hints and location. Before uploading a list, also assess whether transferring that
  data to a platform abroad is compliant under KVKK, Türkiye's data protection law.
</Callout>

## How billing and budgets work

The campaign objective also sets the billing model, and
[the objective cannot be changed after the campaign is created](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001210-create-campaigns-for-chatgpt-ads).

| Objective | Billing | When it fits |
|---|---|---|
| Views | Per 1,000 impressions (CPM) | Awareness, a new brand |
| Clicks | Per valid click (CPC) | Site traffic, a first test |
| Conversions | Per click (oCPC) or per impression (oCPM); no payment per conversion | A sale or form goal with tracking in place |

The daily budget works as a seven-day average target: spend on a single day can reach twice the
daily budget, but the seven-day total does not exceed seven times it. The minimum daily budget
depends on the billing currency, for example USD 25 or EUR 15. The Turkish lira is not in OpenAI's
table, so lira accounts should check the amount in the panel.

## How large is the audience in Türkiye

An ad channel is only as valuable as its audience. TurkStat's
[AI Statistics, published on 2 October 2026](https://bloomberght.com/turkiye-de-yapay-zeka-kullanimi-yaklasik-iki-kat-artti-3790100),
gives a clear picture: the share of people aged 16-74 using generative AI rose from 19.2% to 37.6%
in one year. The share of enterprises using any AI technology also rose from 7.5% to 14%.

## Ads do not change the answer: where GEO fits

OpenAI's principle is clear: [ads do not influence ChatGPT's answers](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt),
they run on systems separate from the chat model, and advertisers cannot shape responses.
Advertisers also do not see users' chats, memories or personal details; they only receive
aggregated data such as impressions and clicks.

That means two separate jobs:

- **Appearing inside the answer** is organic work: your content, your structured data and how your
  brand is cited in trusted sources decide it. We cover this in detail in our
  [SEO to GEO post](/en/blog/search-visibility-in-the-ai-era-seo-to-geo).
- **Appearing below the answer** is paid work: budget, context hints, ad copy and conversion
  tracking decide it.

<Callout type="key" title="Ads do not replace organic visibility">
  If your name does not come up when a user asks ChatGPT for the "best" option, the ad below the
  answer does not fix that; it only opens a second door next to it. The two complement each other,
  but one should not be funded out of the other's budget.
</Callout>

## Measurement: do not start without conversion tracking

[Conversion-optimised campaigns](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001412-conversion-optimized-campaigns)
require conversion tracking on the ad account: the Conversions API, the JavaScript pixel, or both.
Only standard events such as a purchase, sign-up or lead submission are supported for now; custom
events are not. OpenAI also recommends enabling automatic advanced matching.

In its own announcement, OpenAI reports
[an e-commerce advertiser achieving 3x return on ad spend over 28 days](https://openai.com/index/expanding-access-to-ai-with-chatgpt-ads/),
and a technology partner finding that more than 80% of ad-driven traffic came from new customers.
These are single examples chosen by the platform itself, not a general average. For a channel in
beta, the right comparison is your own site's conversion data.

<Callout type="tip" title="Adding a pixel affects your privacy notice too">
  When a new tracking pixel or server-side conversion integration is added to a site, the cookie
  policy and privacy notice should be updated as well, with a consent mechanism where required. Plan
  this work before you set up tracking.
</Callout>

## Who should test it now

| Situation | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Your product or service is a decision people ask AI about (software, education, travel, B2B services) | Test with a small clicks campaign |
| Your conversion tracking already works and you have profitable Google Ads campaigns | Try a conversions campaign with a separate test budget |
| Your audience is mostly aged 18-34 | The channel's audience fits; try image-supported units |
| You sell only locally, in a small area | Wait for now; Google Maps and local search come first |
| You are in a restricted area such as health or politics | Ads are not shown in these conversations; not a fit |
| You have no conversion tracking | Set up measurement first; do not test a channel you cannot measure |

## Checklist for your first campaign

1. **Open the account in the company's name.** Ads do not run until the legal entity, billing
   address and logo are complete.
2. **Choose the conversion event.** A purchase, sign-up or form submission; a frequent event gives
   optimisation more signal.
3. **Set up tracking.** Install the pixel or the Conversions API, verify it with a test event and
   update your privacy notice.
4. **Choose the objective deliberately.** It cannot be changed later; if unsure, start with clicks.
5. **Write context hints from the customer's question.** Describe the problem people ask AI about,
   not the product name.
6. **Match the landing page to the answer.** The user arrives after reading an answer; the page
   should continue that question directly.
7. **Keep the budget separate and give it time.** Work with a fixed test budget, without taking from
   channels that work, until you have enough data to decide.

## Conclusion

ChatGPT ads are a real but early opportunity for businesses in Türkiye. The audience is growing fast,
and the ad appears at the very moment a user is researching a topic. On the other hand, the platform
is in beta, there is no keyword control and independent performance data does not exist yet. The
right step is a small test with measurement in place, run separately from your organic visibility
work.
