Cookies are small text files stored in your browser when you visit a website. Similar technologies such as session storage (sessionStorage) are also used to keep information in your browser. This policy explains which of these technologies are used on the Piton Studios website (the "Site") and for what purpose.

## In short

- The Site **does not use advertising, tracking, profiling or social media cookies.**
- The only cookie used is an essential session cookie that remembers your language choice and is deleted when you close your browser.
- The tools we use for visit statistics and performance measurement do not use cookies.

As also stated in the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority's Guide on Cookie Practices, cookies that are strictly necessary for the operation of a website or for providing a function explicitly requested by the user are not subject to explicit consent. Since the Site uses no non-essential cookies, no cookie consent banner is displayed.

## Cookies and similar technologies we use

| Name | Type | Purpose | Duration | Party |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `NEXT_LOCALE` | Essential cookie | Remembers the language version you chose. Created only when you open a page in a language different from your browser's language setting. | Session; deleted when the browser is closed | First party |
| Loading screen flag | Session storage (sessionStorage) | Prevents the intro animation from being shown again in the same session | Deleted when the browser tab is closed | First party |

Neither of these records contains information that identifies you, and neither is shared with third parties.

## Measurement and performance tools

We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand which pages are visited and how quickly they load. These tools do not write cookies or local storage entries to your browser. Data such as the page viewed, referring site, country, device and browser type, and page performance measurements are processed as aggregate statistics. Details of this processing are available in our [Privacy Policy and Personal Data Notice](/en/privacy).

## Third-party links

The Site contains links to third-party pages such as WhatsApp, our social media accounts and live project websites. These links do not load any cookies when the Site is opened. When you click a link and go to that site, its own cookie and privacy policies apply.

## How to manage cookies

You can view, delete or block cookies and site data in your browser settings. If you delete or block the `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie, the Site selects the language based on your browser's language setting. If you clear session storage, the intro animation is shown again. In both cases, the Site's other functions are not affected.

## Changes

If a new cookie or similar technology is added to the Site, this policy will be updated and, where required, your consent will be obtained before use. The current version is always published on this page, and the update date at the top of the page is changed.
