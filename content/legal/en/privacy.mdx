## Purposes and legal bases for processing your data

**Communication and proposal processes.** We process the information you provide through the contact form, email, WhatsApp or phone in order to respond to your request, understand your project, prepare a proposal and communicate with you. The legal basis is that processing is directly related to the establishment of a contract, pursuant to Article 5(2)(c) of KVKK, and our legitimate interest in being able to respond to your request, pursuant to Article 5(2)(f).

**Security of the Site.** Your IP address is used to limit the number of submissions within a given period, in order to prevent misuse of the form and automated submissions. Our hosting service provider also keeps technical connection logs for the operation and security of the Site. The legal basis is our legitimate interest pursuant to Article 5(2)(f) of KVKK.

**Site performance and statistics.** We use Vercel Web Analytics and Vercel Speed Insights to understand which pages are visited and how fast pages load. These tools do not use cookies; visitors are distinguished by a value that is not intended to identify you and that expires within a short period. The legal basis is our legitimate interest in improving the Site, pursuant to Article 5(2)(f) of KVKK. For details, see the [Cookie Policy](/en/cookies) page.

**Legal obligations.** In the event of law-based requests from competent authorities or possible disputes, we may process your data in order to fulfill our legal obligation pursuant to Article 5(2)(ç) of KVKK, and to establish, exercise or protect a right, pursuant to Article 5(2)(e).

We do not use your contact information to send marketing communications without your explicit consent, and we do not sell your personal data. No decision affecting you is made about you based solely on automated systems.

## Your rights under KVKK

Pursuant to Article 11 of KVKK, by applying to the data controller you have the right to:

1. Learn whether your personal data is being processed,
2. Request information about your personal data if it has been processed,
3. Learn the purpose of processing your personal data and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose,
4. Know the third parties to whom your personal data is transferred domestically or abroad,
5. Request correction of your personal data if it has been processed incompletely or incorrectly,
6. Request the deletion or destruction of your personal data within the framework of the conditions set out in Article 7 of KVKK,
7. Request that correction, deletion and destruction actions be notified to the third parties to whom your personal data has been transferred,
8. Object to the emergence of a result against you by analyzing the processed data exclusively through automated systems,
9. Request compensation for damages if you suffer harm due to the unlawful processing of your personal data.

## How to apply

To exercise your rights, you may submit your application in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles for Applications to the Data Controller, through the following means:

- In writing, by a wet-signed petition together with identity-verifying documents, to the address <ControllerAddress />,
- Signed with a secure electronic signature or mobile signature, or from the email address you have previously notified to us and which is on our records, to <LegalEmail />.

In your application, you must state your name and surname, signature, your Turkish ID number if you are a citizen of the Republic of Turkey (or your nationality and passport number if you are a foreign national), the address to which notice should be sent, your email address and phone number if available, and the subject of your request.

Your application will be concluded free of charge as soon as possible, depending on the nature of the request, and within thirty days at the latest. If the transaction also requires an additional cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board. If your application is rejected, if you find the response inadequate, or if a response is not given within the time limit, you may file a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board pursuant to Article 14 of KVKK.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may also submit your requests regarding your rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) through the same channels.
